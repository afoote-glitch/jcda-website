Building Stronger Communities Across Jamaica

The Jamaica Community Development Association (JCDA) empowers communities through educational programmes, youth initiatives, environmental projects, and meaningful outreach activities.

Welcome to JCDA

A non-profit dedicated to sustainable community development across Jamaica.

Our Mission

To improve the quality of life in Jamaican communities by delivering impactful educational programmes, youth development initiatives, environmental conservation projects, and community outreach activities.

Our Vision

A Jamaica where every community has the tools, knowledge, and support needed to thrive — economically, socially, and environmentally — for generations to come.

Our Approach

We work hand-in-hand with local leaders, schools, churches, and residents to design programmes that address real needs and create lasting positive change.

Green Jamaica Reforestation Drive

In partnership with local schools and community groups, we are planting native trees across degraded hillsides in St. Ann and Clarendon. Over 12,000 seedlings have already been planted, with a target of 25,000 by the end of the year.
