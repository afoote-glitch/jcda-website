About JCDA

Our story, our people, and the values that guide everything we do.

Our History

The Jamaica Community Development Association was founded in 2012 by a group of educators, social workers, and community leaders who recognised the urgent need for coordinated, grassroots development efforts across rural and urban Jamaica.

What began as weekend literacy classes in a church hall in Spanish Town has grown into a national organisation running programmes in education, youth empowerment, environmental restoration, and community infrastructure support.

Today JCDA partners with schools, local government, churches, and international development agencies to deliver sustainable change.

Vision, Mission & Values

The principles that shape our daily work and long-term goals.

Vision

A Jamaica where every community is equipped with the knowledge, resources, and opportunities needed to thrive for generations to come.

Mission

To improve lives through educational programmes, youth initiatives, environmental projects, and community outreach that are community-led and results-focused.

Our Core Values

Community First We listen before we act and design programmes with the people they serve.
Integrity Transparency and accountability guide every decision and partnership.
Empowerment We build capacity so communities can sustain progress long after we leave.
Sustainability Environmental and social responsibility are built into every project.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the growth of JCDA.

2012

JCDA founded in Spanish Town. First literacy and after-school programme launched.

2015

Expanded to three parishes. Youth leadership training programme begins.

2018

Green Jamaica reforestation project starts. First 5,000 trees planted.

2021

Opened two community resource centres. Digital skills training introduced.

2024–2026

National scale-up. Over 12,000 trees planted, 3,800+ youth trained, and active programmes in eight parishes.
