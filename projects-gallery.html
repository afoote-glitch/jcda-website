Current & Recent Projects

A selection of programmes currently underway or recently completed.

🌳
Environment

Green Jamaica Reforestation

Planting native trees on degraded land in St. Ann and Clarendon. Target: 25,000 seedlings by end of 2026. Over 12,400 already in the ground.

📖
Education

After-School Literacy & STEM

Weekly classes in reading, numeracy, and basic coding for primary and secondary students in Spanish Town and Portmore.

👥
Youth

Youth Leadership Pipeline

Training young people in leadership, project management, and civic engagement. Graduates go on to lead local initiatives.

🏠
Community

Community Resource Centres

Supporting the upgrade and operation of 18 community centres that provide meeting space, training rooms, and internet access.
